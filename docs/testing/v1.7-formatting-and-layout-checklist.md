# v1.7 — formatting in the box, and the window in three columns

Fifteen minutes, and the last row is the only one that needs a second machine. Nothing about rooms, sharing or files
changed; the input area, the tools on it and the shape of the window did.

| # | step | expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open a room and look at the window. | **Three columns**: Rooms on the left, Members on the right, the conversation down the middle. The tool strip is above the message box and **inside** the middle column - it does not run over the rooms list or the members list. |
| 2 | Type `ordinary`, highlight `ordinary`, click **B**, send. | The word arrives **bold**, in your transcript and in the other machine's. The words round it are ordinary, and so is the `[time] name` headline over the message. |
| 3 | Click into your bold word with the caret, then out of it again. | The **B** button lights when the caret is in bold and goes flat when it leaves. The buttons tell you where you are, not where you last clicked. |
| 4 | Highlight a word, click **B**, then click **I** (same selection). | It comes out **bold italic**. Losing the bold here is the old RichTextBox trap and would be a regression. |
| 5 | Click **Aa ▼**. | A menu drops down with **Small / Normal / Large**, and each of the three words is written at its own size - the small one really is smaller. The one the caret is sitting in has the tick. |
| 6 | Pick **Large**, write a word, send. Then put the caret in it and open the menu again. | The tool is now **drawn large**, the message arrives large, and the menu says **Large** with the tick. Small is a footnote and large is emphasis; both readable. |
| 7 | Click **A ▼**. | The **whole palette** is there - red, orange, green, blue, purple, pink, automatic - and each colour is written in itself, so you are not reading a name and hoping. |
| 8 | Pick a colour, send; then pick **Automatic**, send. | The first arrives in that colour. The second carries **no colour at all**: it looks exactly like a message would have before v1.7 existed, in the reader's own colours. |
| 9 | Bold a word, send it, then type a plain sentence. | The plain sentence is **plain**. Emphasis that survives the send it was typed for is how a chat turns into a ransom note. |
| 10 | Look at the two buttons at the right end of the strip. | One is a **face** and one is a **sheet of paper**, and both are whole: the face is not cut across the top or the chin, and the page has its fold. No words on either - hover, and the tooltip says what each one is. |
| 11 | Open the emoji table and compare a face in it with the same face in a message you send. | Same face, **complete in both** - nothing missing at the edge of the picture, in the toolbar, the table or the transcript. Faces are slightly smaller than they were, and all of each one is there. |
| 12 | Press the face button, pick a face. | The table opens over the input area, the face lands **at the caret** in the box, and the table closes. Pressing Escape leaves the box exactly as it was. |
| 13 | Copy a sentence out of Word or a web page - one with a colour and a background - and press **Ctrl+V** in the box. | **Only the words arrive.** No fonts, no colours, no grey background, no size change. The box is for a chat message. |
| 14 | Press Print Screen (so the clipboard holds a picture, not words), then **Ctrl+V**. | Nothing is pasted and the status bar says so, naming the **page** button as the way to send a picture. |
| 15 | Open the window on a machine that already has something on the clipboard, then enter a room. | The message box is **empty**. Entering a room must never paste what was left on the clipboard. |
| 16 | Type or paste well over 500 characters, including a few faces like 🙂. | The box keeps **500 characters** (a face is one, not two), the cut lands on a whole character, and the status bar says the rest did not fit. |
| 17 | Drag the window down to its smallest size. | The tools stay on **one row** and none of them sits on top of another. Click **Underline**: it underlines - it does not open the emoji table. |
| 18 | Drag the window taller. | The **transcript** grows. The tool strip and the message box stay the same height, and Send stays beside the box, on the box's row. |
| 19 | As host: resize the window narrow, then look at the rooms header. | The **New** / **Delete** buttons stay inside the Rooms column, over the list - never over the transcript. |
| 20 | A screen reader, or just Tab round the strip. | Every tool says what it is: the toggles by their letters, the two menus by **font size** / **font colour**, the face as **Emoji** and the page as **Share a picture or a file**. |
| 21 | Second machine: send formatted text both ways, and switch rooms so the transcript is rebuilt from history. | Both windows put the **same ink** on the screen - same bold, same size, same colour - and the formatting survives the rebuild. An ordinary message from the old version still arrives exactly as it always did. |
